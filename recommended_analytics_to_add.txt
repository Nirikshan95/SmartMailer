Recommended Analytics to Add
Based on your email automation platform, here are valuable insights you could add:

1. Email Validation Metrics
Bounce Rate by Domain: Track which email domains have highest bounce rates
Validation Success Rate: Percentage of emails passing validation
Invalid Email Reasons: Breakdown of why emails fail (disposable, no MX, SMTP failure, etc.)
Domain Health Score: Track problematic domains over time
2. Delivery Performance
Delivery Rate Trends: Track successful delivery over time
Failed Email Analysis: Categorize failures (daily limit, hourly limit, SMTP errors)
Peak Sending Times: Identify best times for email delivery based on success rates
Average Response Time: SMTP handshake and delivery times
3. Campaign Intelligence
Campaign Comparison: Side-by-side comparison of multiple campaigns
Best Performing Campaigns: Rank campaigns by success rate
Campaign Duration: Time to complete campaigns
Retry Analysis: Track emails that needed multiple attempts
4. Resource Usage
Daily/Hourly Limit Tracking: Visual gauge showing how close you are to limits
Sending Velocity Over Time: Track emails per hour/day trends
Peak Usage Hours: Heatmap of when most emails are sent
Quota Utilization: Percentage of daily/hourly limits used
5. List Quality Metrics
List Health Score: Overall quality of prospect lists
Duplicate Detection: Track duplicate emails across campaigns
Corporate vs Personal Domains: Distribution analysis
Lenient Provider Usage: Track usage of Gmail, Outlook, etc.
6. Predictive Analytics
Estimated Completion Time: Predict when campaigns will finish
Success Probability: ML-based prediction of email delivery success
Optimal Send Times: Recommend best times based on historical data
Budget Forecasting: Estimate costs based on sending patterns
7. Real-time Monitoring
Live Campaign Dashboard: Real-time updates during active sends
Error Alerts: Immediate notification of issues
Rate Limit Warnings: Alert when approaching limits
System Health: Monitor SMTP connection status
