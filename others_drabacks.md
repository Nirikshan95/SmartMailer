Here are the **faults and drawbacks of most free mail merge add-ons and scripts** for Gmail, which you should address if you want to build a more impactful bulk email sender:

**1. Low Daily Sending Limits**
- Free plans restrict usage to 20–75 emails per day, far below Gmail’s own 500/day limit for personal accounts.
- Many tools require a paid plan for larger campaigns.[1][2][3]

**2. Manual Re-Running for Over-Limit Sheets**
- No queuing or auto-resume for emails over the quota; user must manually re-run after the quota resets.
- Risk of duplicates or missing recipients if not tracked carefully.

**3. Lack of Delivery Tracking and Analytics**
- Free plans rarely offer robust open/click tracking, bounce detection, or delivery reporting.
- Users can’t see campaign performance or email engagement.

**4. No Native Scheduling**
- Most add-ons can’t schedule emails to be sent later or at staggered intervals—everything sends immediately, risking hitting limits or spam filters.

**5. Limited Personalization**
- Only basic fields (name, email, or a few columns) are supported.
- Advanced personalization (conditional logic, images, file attachments per user) is restricted or unavailable.

**6. Gmail Throttling and Spam Risks**
- Bulk mail sent quickly may be flagged as spam or throttle your Gmail account; most tools don’t pace sending or rotate accounts/IPs.

**7. Privacy and Security Concerns**
- Add-ons may require broad permissions and may store your data remotely (risk of data leaks if not transparent).

**8. Lack of Unsubscribe Handling**
- No easy way to add/manage “unsubscribe” links, risking compliance issues (CAN-SPAM/GDPR).

**9. Poor Error Handling or Feedback**
- Errors (bounces, rejections, Google quota errors) are not always clearly surfaced to the user.

**10. Attachments and Inline Images**
- Limits or complexity in adding personalized attachments or images (often paid-only feature).

***

If you want to create a **more impactful bulk email sender**, focus on:
- Allowing higher batch sizes (up to Gmail’s limits),
- Seamless queueing and auto-resume over multiple days,
- Built-in analytics,
- Rich personalization,
- Scheduling and throttling,
- Secure data handling,
- Easy unsubscribe links and compliance support,
- Clear feedback and error management,
- Convenient attachment support.

Addressing these drawbacks can help make your tool stand out and offer real value over existing free solutions.[2][3][1]
